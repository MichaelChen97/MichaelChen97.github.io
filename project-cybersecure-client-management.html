01 What it does

Five CRUD modules over a relational database.

  • Five modules for clients, accounts, services, subscriptions and incidents, each with create, read, update and delete.
  • Relational tables in SQL Server, with stored procedures, views and triggers to keep the data consistent.
  • Validation and form navigation, so that incomplete or invalid records are caught before they are saved.
