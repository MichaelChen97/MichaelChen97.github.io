Work/Livestock Management

.NET MAUI · sole author

The farm maths, done for you.

A desktop app for a farm’s cows and sheep. It keeps the records, previews income and profit as you type, works out each animal’s cost and the farm’s tax, and answers questions like “which animals earn the most?” with queries and charts.

01 What it does

Records, calculations, questions.

  • Add, update and delete cows and sheep with their expense, weight, colour and milk or wool output.
  • The Add form previews income and profit as you type; cost per animal and the government tax totals appear on the Dashboard, Analysis and Query screens.
  • Query by type and colour, sort by profit, and forecast what buying more animals would earn.
  • A dashboard of Microcharts charts that summarise the whole farm.

03 How it is built

Layers over SQLite.

  • Models, repositories and services over a local SQLite database (sqlite-net), seeded from a bundled synthetic database on first run.
  • The Dashboard uses a dependency-injected view model (CommunityToolkit.Mvvm); the other pages use code-behind.
  • Queries and statistics use LINQ (filtering, sums, averages) in the services and the query and analysis pages.
  • Charts are drawn with Microcharts on SkiaSharp.

04 Calculations

From individual records to farm-wide totals.

The app applies the same rules across its forms, queries and charts. Daily income uses milk yield × 9.4 for cows or wool yield × 6.2 for sheep. Tax is 0.02 × weight; profit deducts expenses and tax from income.

The live preview makes those relationships visible before Save. Filters and forecasts then turn individual records into totals that can be compared across the farm.
