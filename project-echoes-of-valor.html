Echoes of Valor

A top-down pixel-art RPG made by a student team in Godot 4.4. I laid out the first versions of two of its levels: the Inner Forest and the Outer Temple.

01 The game

From village to the Inner Sanctum.

After a splash screen, the main menu and four story panels, the hero sets out from an intro village and plays through four levels in order: Level 1, the Outskirts; Level 2, the Inner Forest; Level 3, the Outer Temple; and Level 4, the Boss arena, which Level 3’s exit signposts as the Inner Sanctum. There he faces the Bringer of Death. Shrines are purified along the way, and each purified shrine gives a gem that doubles health and attack. Defeating the Boss leads to the good ending; any death leads to the bad ending. The game has four-direction movement, a dash with stamina, and combat with critical hits and knockback.

02 What I built

Two levels’ worth of ground to walk on.

  • Level 2, Inner Forest: the first-pass tile layout (the paths, stones and water layers) and the scaffolding for the shrine, portal, signpost and level transition. These layers survive unchanged in the final game.
  • Level 3, Outer Temple: the first-pass tile layout (grass, water, paths and stones), also unchanged in the final game.
  • An early Level 1 prototype, later replaced by the team, and a merge-conflict fix.

The team lead populated the levels and built the systems: enemies, the Boss and its arena, combat, dialogue, the story and menus, the HUD and touch controls, the settings and the Android export.
